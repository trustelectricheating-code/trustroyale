## Processing

FFmpeg 9.0.2 trimmed, mixed and normalised all sources. The sprite is encoded as Opus WebM at 96 kb/s with a 128 kb/s MP3 fallback. Music is encoded as Opus WebM at 80 kb/s with a 112 kb/s MP3 fallback.
