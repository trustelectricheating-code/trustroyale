## Shared constraints

Dress him as the approved croupier: red dealer visor with black brim, black bow tie, black or deep-red waistcoat with gold buttons over a white shirt, and a clearly visible circular Union Jack medallion below the bow tie. Show exactly two arms, two paws, two legs and one tail. No extra limbs, cropped body parts, background, text or watermark. Use a transparent background and warm Monte Carlo Velvet casino lighting.

## Poses

- **Wave:** Keith stands and waves one open paw in welcome; his other paw rests naturally at his side.
- **Point:** Keith points clearly toward the rules or machine while his other paw rests at his waist.
- **Prizes:** Keith holds a blank deep-red, gold-trimmed prize placard with both paws; the medallion remains visible above the placard.
- **Three chips:** Keith presents exactly three approved T chips—one white, one red and one navy—with two chips fanned in one paw and one in the other. Each chip has a crisp white T. No third paw.
- **Good luck:** Keith gives one clear thumbs-up with a warm, encouraging smile.
- **Celebrate:** Keith raises both paws in a joyful but gentle win celebration.
- **Last Chance:** Keith presents one gold casino chip on an open paw and gives an encouraging thumbs-up with the other paw.
